Security note: webhook and Slack URLs are fetched by the server and can reach internal hosts (SSRF). Only configure destinations you trust. Outbound requests use a 10s timeout, refuse redirects, and allow only http/https schemes. Generic-webhook deliveries are signed with HMAC-SHA256 over the request body (header X-Spire-Signature: sha256=…) plus a X-Spire-Timestamp to let receivers reject replays.
